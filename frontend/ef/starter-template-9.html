Why East Founder?

Seed and early stage funding are ahead in the West (US and Europe) with new innovative investment solutions and transparent relations between entrepreneurs and investors. Unfortunately if you are based in the East, it is hard to take advantage of these game changers for your startup.

EastFunding aims to change this by helping Eastern entrepreneurs from Asia to Middle East to:

  • Get funding from accredited investors of all around the world
  • Hire awesome talents
  • Connect with Corporate companies for business and M&A
  • Find Incubator, Accelerator or co-Working space
  • Build relationships with peers, investors and talents
