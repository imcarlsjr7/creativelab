About Me

Hi! I'm Carl Caralos, Web Designer from the Philippines and currently working in Singapore. I have 15+ years of experience in the design industry with my aim to create a website that is aesthetically appealing, usable and useful for people. I help startups and businesses to create good design and enjoyable experience that align with end-user needs and business goals. I'm inspired by designing websites that are aesthetically appealing and intuitive to use.

I have worked with various clients from Australia, US, New Zealand, UK, Ireland, France, Canada, Russia, Spain, Sweden, Germany, Switzerland, Dubai, Singapore and also with some brands including SugarCRM, Pearson and HP. I have experience in Photoshop, Illustrator, XD, Sketch App, and Invision.

Here's my web design process and workflow: 1. Gathering and understanding the necessary information and data 2. Researching on competitors and looking for design inspirations 3. Creating wireframe and sitemap 4. Designing high-fidelity mockups/layouts 5. Creating interactive prototype 6. Usability testing, gathering user feedback, design iterations and validation
