About Me

Hi! I'm Carl, UI/UX Designer and Front-end Developer from the Philippines. I have 7+ years of experience in the Design Industry with my aim to create a product that is aesthetically appealing, usable and useful for people. I have to understand the User-Centered Design process, learn and adapt the various design disciplines in User Interface Design, User Experience Design, Visual Design, Interaction Design and Information Architecture. I help startups and businesses to create good design and enjoyable experience that align with end-user needs and business goals. I'm inspired by designing products that are aesthetically appealing and intuitive to use.

I have worked with various clients from Australia, US, New Zealand, UK, Ireland, France, Canada, Russia, Spain, Sweden, Germany, Switzerland, Dubai, Singapore and also with some brands including SugarCRM, Pearson and HP. I have experienced in Photoshop, Illustrator, Sketch, Brackets, CSS3/HTML5, LESS, Bootstrap, GruntJS, GIT, User Interface Design, Icon Design, Logo Design, Front-end Development and basic working knowledge with JQuery. Here's my design process and workflow: 1. Gathering and understanding the necessary information and data 2. Researching on competitors and design inspiration 3. Creating wireframes 4. Designing high-fidelity mockups 5. Creating interactive prototype 6. Usability testing, gathering user feedback, design iterations and product validation 7. Front-end development
