About Me

Hi! I'm Carls Jr, a Senior User Interface / Product designer and Front-end developer from the Philippines with 7+ years of experience. I help startups and businesses to create an appropriate designs and experiences that align with user’s needs and goals. I'm inspired by designing products that do not only focus on the visual interface designs but also works. I graduated BS – Computer Science at La Salle University. I'm available for freelance work in User Interface design for both web and mobile (iOS & Android), User Experience design, Logo design and Front-end development.

I have work with various clients from Australia, US, New Zealand, UK, Ireland, France, Canada, Russia, Spain, Sweden, Germany, Switzerland, Dubai and Singapore. I can design clean, minimalist, corporate and responsive websites that are W3C compliant. I can also create brand logo, poster, business card and other materials for both web & print. I have experience in using Photoshop, Illustrator, Sketch, coding CSS3 / HTML5 with LESS (responsive / adaptive) and have some basic knowledge with JQuery on designer’s point of view.
